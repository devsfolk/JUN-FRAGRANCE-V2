## Who we are

Jun.fragrance is a perfume brand from Karachi, Pakistan, started in 2026. We are a small, independent business and we are not connected to any famous perfume house.

The setup is simple on purpose. Every perfume comes in a 50 ml bottle and costs Rs. 1,999. Pick any three and the price is Rs. 4,500. You order on WhatsApp, talk to us directly, and get your questions answered before you pay.

## What we sell

Our range is split into men's, women's and unisex perfumes. Each one has its own name and a mood to match: some cool and fresh, some warm and spicy, some soft and floral. Where we have the notes, we list them on the product page. Where we do not, we describe the mood and leave the guessing out.

Pakistani weather changes how a perfume behaves, so we write with real days in mind: the office, college, weddings and evenings out. Our perfumes last approximately 6 to 8 hours, though skin, heat and the number of sprays can change that.

## What "inspired by" means

These are perfumes with a similar scent style, not the original perfumes. Jun.fragrance is an independent brand and is not affiliated with those brands.

## Delivery, payment and returns

We deliver across Pakistan in 3 to 5 days, between 12 PM and 9 PM. Delivery is free within Karachi. Outside Karachi, the charge is between Rs. 200 and Rs. 250. You can pay by cash on delivery or online. For online payment, we share our account details with you on WhatsApp.

We have a 7-day return policy, and exchanges are available. Exchange delivery charges are paid by the customer, even in Karachi. Exact conditions are confirmed on WhatsApp.
