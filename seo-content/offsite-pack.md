### What needs a physical address (read this first)

Google Business Profile needs an address to create and verify a profile. Decide which case is yours:

- You have a shop, studio or pickup point customers can visit: add that address. Customers can see it.
- You work from home or only deliver: choose a "service area" business, hide your street address and set your service area to Karachi (and any other cities you serve). Google still needs your address privately for verification.
- You do not want to give any address: skip Google Business Profile for now. Everything else in this pack works without it.

These items need an address: creating the profile, verification (Google may ask for a video, a postcard, a call or a code), opening hours and the map pin. All the text below can be used once you have a profile.

## 4. Eight realistic free places for links and mentions in Pakistan

1. **Instagram profile link**: put https://www.junfragrance.com in your bio link. Post one journal tip a week and link to it in the story.
2. **Facebook page**: add the website, WhatsApp number and a short description to your page's About section. Share each new journal post there.
3. **TikTok profile**: once you create the account, add the website link if the profile allows it, or put the URL in your bio text.
4. **WhatsApp Business profile**: switch to the WhatsApp Business app, add your website, description and delivery hours, and list your perfumes in the catalog. Customers who find you on WhatsApp then see the website.
5. **Google Business Profile**: the most useful free listing for local searches in Karachi. Add the website, categories and photos once you have an address or service area.
6. **Free business directories in Pakistan**: search for free Pakistani business directories and add your name, city, phone and website exactly as they appear on your site. Use the same details everywhere. Skip any directory that asks for money for a link.
7. **Local bloggers and creators**: message small Karachi lifestyle and fashion creators. Offer to send a bottle in exchange for an honest opinion, and ask them to say that it was sent free. Do not tell them what to say.
8. **Karachi buying and selling Facebook groups**: read the group rules, then post a short, honest introduction with your price and WhatsApp number. Do not post the same message again and again.

Do not buy links, paid review packages or "SEO backlink" offers. They can get your site and profiles penalised.

## 5. 30-day checklist

### Week 1: Set up the basics

- [ ] Open Google Search Console, add junfragrance.com and submit the sitemap.
- [ ] Import the `seo-content/` files through your coding agent and check a few pages in the browser.
- [ ] Create or update your Instagram and Facebook profile with the new bio and website link.
- [ ] Switch to WhatsApp Business and fill in the profile and catalog.

### Week 2: Local presence

- [ ] Create your Google Business Profile (decide on address or service area first).
- [ ] Add 8 photos: bottles, a parcel, a hand holding one.
- [ ] Post your first Google update and your first 3 Instagram posts.
- [ ] Create a TikTok account and post 2 short videos.

### Week 3: Get honest reviews

- [ ] Send the review message to 5 happy customers.
- [ ] Share the first two journal posts on Instagram and Facebook.
- [ ] Add your website and number to 2 free Pakistani business directories.
- [ ] Reply to every WhatsApp message and comment, even short ones.

### Week 4: Look and repeat

- [ ] Check Search Console: which pages have impressions, and which are not indexed yet.
- [ ] Message 3 local creators or bloggers.
- [ ] Share one journal post a week for the next month.
- [ ] Note which posts bring WhatsApp messages and do more of those.

Be patient. SEO normally takes weeks or months to show up, and a brand-new site has no history yet. The steps above help it get found sooner, and the WhatsApp messages you get from Instagram and Google often come before any search traffic.
