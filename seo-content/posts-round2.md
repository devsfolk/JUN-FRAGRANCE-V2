seo_description: Learn where to apply perfume, how to start with a light amount, and how to wear it comfortably through a busy day in Pakistan.
--- content ---
You are nearly out the door for class, and the bottle is already in your hand. A few sprays seem harmless, but in a small car or a crowded lift, a little can travel farther than expected. Application is mostly about placement and restraint. You can add more later; you cannot take a cloud of perfume back off the air.

## Start with clean, dry skin

Apply perfume after washing and drying, before getting dressed if you can. Damp skin can make the spray run, while sweat or another strong fragrance can alter what you smell. There is no need to make a ritual out of it. Pick the areas you want, spray from a comfortable distance, and let the liquid settle on its own.

If your skin feels dry, a plain, unscented moisturiser may help the fragrance sit more evenly. Keep the amount modest and let it absorb first. A strongly scented lotion adds another smell, so the result may not resemble the perfume by itself. If you prefer not to apply fragrance to skin, fabric is an option, with a little care.

## Choose a few places

### Pulse areas

The wrists, sides of the neck and inner elbows are easy places to reach. You do not need to cover every point. Choose one or two areas and see how the scent feels in your normal day. The chest under clothing gives a quieter effect, while the neck may be more noticeable to people standing close.

### Clothing and hair

Fabric can hold a fragrance differently from skin, but some materials may stain. Try a small spray on a hidden seam and let it dry before wearing a light or delicate garment. Avoid spraying jewellery, leather or a special outfit directly. If you choose to scent your hair, use a light touch and keep the bottle away from your face and eyes.

### Areas to skip

Do not spray into your eyes, mouth or onto irritated skin. Avoid rubbing your wrists together after spraying. It is a familiar habit, but it can make the first impression feel different from simply letting the perfume dry. Let each application settle before adding another.

## How much should you use?

There is no single spray count that suits every perfume, person and room. Start with one spray in a shared space, then wait a few minutes before deciding whether you want another. For a wedding or an open outdoor gathering, you might choose a little more than for a small office, but keep nearby people in mind.

The best check is distance. Ask someone you trust whether they can notice the fragrance when they are standing normally beside you. If it fills a room before you arrive, reduce the amount next time. You can always reapply lightly later. Applying more at the beginning does not make the experience better for everyone around you.

## Fit the application to the day

For a lecture, meeting or bus ride, choose fewer sprays and keep them under clothing if you want a close effect. Before an evening event, a light refresh can be more comfortable than starting heavily in the morning. If you are moving between air conditioning and the heat outside, check how the fragrance feels after the commute before adding more.

If you are trying a new scent, wear it on a normal day first. That gives you a useful sense of how it changes while you work, travel or sit with other people. Browse the [shop](/shop) at your own pace, or look at the [unisex range](/category/unisex) if you are choosing a shared bottle.

## Common application mistakes

Spraying repeatedly because you cannot smell your own fragrance is an easy way to use too much. People become used to a scent while wearing it, so your own impression can fade before the people around you notice it less. Give it time before deciding to top up.

Another mistake is spraying the air and walking through it. Much of the fragrance lands elsewhere, and the amount on you becomes hard to judge. Apply directly to the chosen area instead. Finally, avoid keeping a bottle in a hot vehicle or in direct sun; store it in a cool, dry place at home.

## Who this routine may not suit

If you prefer no fragrance at work, school or home, skipping perfume is a perfectly reasonable choice. Some venues also ask guests not to wear scented products. Follow that request. This guide is for people who want to wear perfume considerately, not a reason to wear it every day.

Perfume does not stay exactly as it was in the first spray. The opening can feel different from the scent that remains after some time.
