## What this page covers

This page explains what personal details Jun.fragrance collects when you order from us, why we collect them and what we do with them.

## What we collect

When you order on WhatsApp, you give us your name, phone number and delivery address, along with the perfumes you want. We collect only what we need to process your order. The website has no customer accounts and does not take payments.

## How we use your details

We use your details only to confirm and process your order, arrange delivery, handle returns and exchanges, and reply to your questions.

## Who sees your details

We do not sell your details, and we do not share them for advertising. We share only what a courier needs to deliver your order, such as your name, phone number and address. WhatsApp is run by a third party, so our chats are also covered by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

## Online payment

If you choose online payment, we share our account details with you in the WhatsApp chat. Please pay only to details we send you in our own chat.

## How long we keep your details

We keep order details only as long as we need them for your order, any return or exchange, and basic business records.

## Your choices

You can message us on WhatsApp to ask what details we hold about you, to correct them, or to ask us to delete them. We will do this unless we still need the details for an order that is not complete or for our records.

## Website data

The website may record basic technical information, such as which pages are visited and what type of device is used, through our hosting or analytics tools. We use this to keep the site working and to understand which pages are useful. This information does not include your name or phone number.

## Changes to this page

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
